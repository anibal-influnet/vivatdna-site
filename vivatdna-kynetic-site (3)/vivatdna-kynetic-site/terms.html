1. What VivatDNA is — and isn't

VivatDNA provides genetic testing and reporting for wellness and informational purposes. Kynetic layers daily, AI-generated suggestions on top of that reporting, informed by your genome, connected wearables, and lab results. Neither product diagnoses disease, prescribes treatment, or replaces a licensed physician. Pharmacogenomic guidance included in your Clinical Overview is educational; any actual prescribing decision should be made by a licensed physician or genetic counselor with your full clinical picture in view.

2. Eligibility

You must be 18 or older to purchase a kit or create an account. This service is not intended for use in a medical emergency — if you are experiencing one, contact emergency services or your physician directly.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information when registering your sample.
  • Use Kynetic's daily guidance as one input among others, not as a stand-alone basis for medical decisions.
  • Consult a physician before making changes to medication, especially anything referencing your pharmacogenomic results.
